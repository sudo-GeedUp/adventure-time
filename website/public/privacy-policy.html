Privacy Policy - Its Adventure Time

Last Updated: July 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Location Data: GPS coordinates for trail tracking and navigation
  • Account Information: Email address and profile information you provide
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App and its features
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and unique device identifiers

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality
  • Track and save your adventure routes
  • Enable community features and sharing
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Improve our services and develop new features
  • Communicate with you about your account

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With service providers who help operate the App (e.g., Firebase for authentication, database, and storage; RevenueCat for subscription management; Sentry for crash reporting; the National Weather Service and OpenWeather for weather data)
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • With your consent for specific purposes

4. Subscriptions

Purchase information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not store your payment information.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information, including encryption and secure data storage. However, no method of transmission is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the App's services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Opt-out of certain data collection
  • Export your data

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App.
